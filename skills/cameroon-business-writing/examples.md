# Examples

## Good — WhatsApp, French

> Bonjour 👋 Le *sac en raphia tissé main* est de retour en stock, à *12 000 FCFA*.
> Répondez *OUI* pour le réserver.

## Good — WhatsApp, English

> Hello! Our *shea butter soap (150 g)* is back, *2 500 FCFA* each.
> Reply *ORDER* and we'll confirm delivery with you.

## Bad

> 🔥🔥 MEGA PROMO!!! Only today, 50% off everything, free delivery everywhere!!! 🔥🔥

Why: invented discount and free delivery, false urgency, too many emojis.
