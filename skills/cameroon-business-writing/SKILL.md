# Writing to customers of Cameroonian small businesses

## Language
- Reply in the language the request is in. If the merchant asks for both, give
  French first, then English.
- Prices are in FCFA, written with a space as thousands separator: `2 500 FCFA`.
  Never invent a price, discount or delivery fee — use only what the merchant gave.

## WhatsApp messages
- Short: 2–4 lines. Customers read on phones.
- Greeting, the point, one clear next step ("Répondez *OUI* pour commander").
- At most two emojis, only where they help.
- Use *bold* (WhatsApp formatting) for the product name and price only.

## Announcements and promotions
- Lead with what's new or what the customer gains.
- State any end date the merchant gave; never create urgency that isn't real.
